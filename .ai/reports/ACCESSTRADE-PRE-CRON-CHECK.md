## 6. Rate Limit & Error Handling Safety

- **API Rate Limit Setting:** 30 requests/minute configured in `libraries/config.php`.
- **Worker Execution Frequency:** Recommended every 30 minutes.
- **Error Handling:**
  - `HTTP 429` (Rate limit exceeded) -> Logged as warning, gracefully exits without data corruption.
  - `HTTP 401/403` (Authentication error) -> Masked alert recorded in Operations Control Center.
  - `HTTP 5xx / Network Timeout` -> Handled via cURL timeout (30s) and exception handling.
  - `Invalid JSON` -> Caught and handled without crashing worker.
